ERA Intelligence Guide

What ERA watches & why it matters
EMA 9 / 20 / 50Trend alignment. ERA uses EMA structure to judge bullish, bearish or sideways conditions.
RSIMomentum and exhaustion. Helps ERA detect strength, weakness, overbought and oversold conditions.
VWAPSession price benchmark. ERA compares price with VWAP to understand intraday positioning.
VolumeParticipation. A volume expansion can validate a move; weak participation can reduce confidence.
Market StructureHH/HL and LH/LL plus BOS/CHOCH help ERA identify trend continuation or a possible shift.
Options IntelligenceStrike, expiry, OI, change in OI, IV, Greeks and PCR help ERA validate an options setup.
Risk EnginePosition size, stop loss, daily loss and exposure checks help prevent unsafe paper trades.
AI ConfidenceERA issues an option trade only when its calculated confidence is above 60%; otherwise it stays in WATCH / NO TRADE.
